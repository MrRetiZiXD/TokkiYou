# Additional terms

TokkiYou is under the GNU General Public License, version 3, which is in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). These are additional terms under its section 7. They apply
to TokkiYou and to every work based on it.

Why they exist, in one paragraph. The project used to be under MIT. People took the
whole mod, moved the author's name into small print at the bottom of a screen
nobody opens, removed the part that asks for support, and shipped it as their
own. That is within MIT and it is not what the licence was for. Section 7 of
the GPL exists for exactly this: it lets an author require that attribution
stay where people can see it.

---

## 1. Keep the author's notices where they are (section 7b)

The mod's own settings screen carries a section that names the author of
TokkiYou, links to the project, and carries the author's donation links. That
section is the **Appropriate Legal Notices** of this program.

If you convey a modified version, you must keep that section:

- reachable from the mod's own settings, not from a submenu two levels deeper;
- in the same place in the list of settings, at the same size as the rows
  around it, with the same prominence;
- with its links intact and working.

You may translate it. You may put your own name and your own links **beside**
the author's, with equal or greater prominence for yourself if you like. You
may not replace the author's, shrink them, hide them, move them into an "About"
page that nothing links to, or make them unreadable.

## 2. Say that it is yours, not the author's (section 7b, 7c)

A modified version must state, in that same section, that it is modified and
that it does not come from the author of TokkiYou. Somebody looking at your build
must be able to tell whose work they are running and whom to blame for it.

## 3. The names are not part of the licence (section 7e)

"TokkiYou" and the mod's icon are the author's. A modified version may
not carry those names or that icon as its own name and face. Saying "based on
TokkiYou" is fine and is encouraged.

## What this does not do

It does not stop you forking, changing, or redistributing anything. It does not
ask for money. It does not forbid removing any feature of the mod other than
the notices above. The GPL's own terms are untouched: the source of a modified
version has to stay open, under the same licence, with these same additional
terms.

## History

Versions up to and including 0.23.2 were released under MIT, and that stands:
a copy you already have under MIT stays under MIT. From 1.24 onward the project
is GPL-3.0 with the terms above. Code contributed to the project before the
change was MIT, which the GPL accepts.

The Material icons in `icons/` are Google's, Apache 2.0, and are not covered by
any of this.
